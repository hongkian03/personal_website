## Contribution

Joined the board to lead the events portfolio. Coordinated logistics, programming, and stakeholder communication for a calendar of cultural and professional events serving 300+ students, working with a 12-person board and a network of campus partners.

## Approach

Planned the calendar as a year-long arc rather than a list of one-offs, so each event built on the previous one and gave regular attendees a reason to keep coming back. Kept close tabs on which formats landed and which didn't, and brought that data into the next planning round.

## Outcome and learning

Delivered 8 events over the year and lifted attendance by 25% against the previous cycle. The biggest personal takeaway was the value of treating events as a system: a good theme, a reliable rhythm, and a clear audience does more than any single well-designed program.
