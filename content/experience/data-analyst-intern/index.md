## Context

In the Spring 2026 semester, I interned for a nonprofit whose main goal is to raise awareness of the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## Reflections

For my internship project, I made a dashboard using Python and Streamlit to viualize the progress made by Vietnam in achieving SDGs 4 (quality education), 8 (decent work and economic growth), and 10 (reduced inequalities). I selected indicators based on coverage, comparability, analytical value, and visualization feasibility. It was quite a hard balance, since data availability was sometimes scarce for seemingly no particular reason. I built a four-tab Streamlit dashboard with eight visualizations. Afterwards, I presented my findings to other interns and my supervisors for feedback.

Besides that project, I also took a self-directed course and certified in Google Analytics. I then cleaned and aggregated Google Analytics and survey data, and built Tableau and Power BI dashboards for the engagement side. That was a valuable experience in data analytics and consumer analytics, as the data I collected was used to improve the organization's websites, such as the one used for signups for our annual virtual race event.

I appreciated the experience working in a big team, for a nonprofit, in data analytics, since at the time I had only recently added Mathematics as a major in addition to Computer Science to explore my interests in statistics and data science. The work was entirely remote, but surprisingly I did not enjoy it that much. I would have much rather worked in an in-person office setting so that I could better network with fellow interns and my supervisors. Given the prevalence of hybrid and work-from-home solutions in the modern workplace, this revelation is perhaps quite important for me in realizing my professional preferences for future work.
