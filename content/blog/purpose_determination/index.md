Over the course of my life, I have embraced and heard various perspectives on how one's purpose in life is determined. Being born and raised in Singapore, I was brought up to prioritize optimizing for one's future career trajectory and maximizing material wealth and income. That collective, national mindset is often cited as a catalyst to Singapore's economic growth, and it is indeed a valid strategy and mental framework. Little room is left for creative or passionate endeavours when most of one's headspace is taken up by pragmatic and practical considerations. Despite developing a strong interest in computer science and deciding to commit to it in university, I cannot deny that a huge reason there was not much pushback from my accountant parents (who chose their path for the successful pursuit of material wealth) was because the field itself promised a stable career.

Attending college in the United States has exposed me to varying perspectives.
